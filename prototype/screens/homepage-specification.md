The homepage establishes NAFSA as a national SPS regulatory information platform.

```

Hero Section
- Authority statement

- Language switch

Quick Access
```

Search-first approach:

"Search regulations, market requirements, laboratories and SPS information"

## Visual Intent

- Government-grade
- Clean
- Trustworthy
