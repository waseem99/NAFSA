# NAFSA Design Tokens

## Purpose

Foundation tokens for the NAFSA Digital Regulatory Platform visual system.

## Brand Direction

Design personality:

Trusted. Transparent. Connected.

The interface should communicate:

- Regulatory authority
- Scientific credibility
- Digital accessibility
- International trade enablement

# Shadow Principles

Use minimal elevation.

Avoid consumer-style cards.

Prefer:
- borders
- whitespace
- subtle depth

---

# Accessibility

Target:

WCAG 2.1 AA

Requirements:

- sufficient contrast
- keyboard navigation
- readable typography
- RTL support
- screen reader compatibility
