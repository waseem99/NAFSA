# NAFSA Homepage Visual Concept

## Objective

Create the first impression of NAFSA as a trusted national SPS regulatory authority.

The homepage should communicate:

- authority
- transparency
- regulatory knowledge
- digital accessibility

---

# Hero Section

Visual direction:

Institutional imagery combined with regulatory search experience.

Components:

- NAFSA identity
- headline
- supporting statement
- universal search
- quick access actions

Example:

"Your trusted source for SPS regulatory information"

Search:
"Search regulations, markets, laboratories and alerts"

---

# Quick Access Modules

Cards:

## Market Access
Find export/import requirements.

## Regulations
Explore SPS regulatory instruments.

## Laboratories
Find accredited laboratories.

## Alerts
View current pest and disease notifications.

---

# Regulatory Intelligence Section

Show:

- latest updates
- important notifications
- regulatory changes

---

# Data & Insights Section

Visual elements:

- statistics cards
- charts
- downloadable reports

---

# Publications Section

Display:

- reports
- guidelines
- manuals
- official documents

---

# PSW Connection Section

Purpose:

Explain ecosystem relationship.

Message:

NAFSA helps users understand SPS requirements.

PSW enables trade transactions.

---

# Visual Tone

Avoid:

- generic agriculture imagery
- marketing-style banners
- excessive decoration

Prefer:

- scientific credibility
- regulatory confidence
- clean information architecture
