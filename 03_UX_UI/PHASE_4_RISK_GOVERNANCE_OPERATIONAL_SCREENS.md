# Phase 4 — Risk, Governance & Operational Experience Screens

## Objective

Extend the NAFSA prototype beyond public information discovery into operational capabilities required by the platform.

## Screens Implemented

### 1. Pest & Disease Alert Dashboard

Purpose:
- Present SPS risk information
- Show severity levels
- Communicate affected areas and recommended actions

### 2. Statistics & Market Intelligence Dashboard

Purpose:
- Provide regulatory insights
- Present platform indicators
- Support stakeholder decision making

### 3. Employee Portal

Purpose:
- Secure employee information access
- Internal notices
- Office orders
- Memorandums
- Training schedules

No HR transaction workflows are included based on client clarification.

### 4. CMS Maker-Checker Workflow

Purpose:
- Demonstrate content governance
- Role-based approval process
- Audit trail readiness

Workflow:

Author → Reviewer → NAFSA Approval → Published

## RFP Alignment

These screens demonstrate:
- regulatory risk communication
- operational governance
- secure internal access
- controlled publishing workflows
