# NAFSA Digital Platform - Proposed Visual Identity System

## Brand Positioning

NAFSA should be positioned as a trusted national regulatory information platform enabling safe trade, transparency and stakeholder access.

## Design Personality

Institutional Modern:
- Government authority
- Digital innovation
- Scientific credibility
- International trade enablement

## Brand Attributes

- Trusted
- Transparent
- Connected
- Secure
- Accessible

## Proposed Colour Direction

Primary: Regulatory Green
- Agriculture
- Safety
- Sustainability

Secondary: Deep Navy
- Authority
- Governance
- Trust

Accent: Regulatory Gold
- Certification
- Standards

Neutral system:
- White
- Light backgrounds
- High contrast text

## Typography

English:
- Inter / Noto Sans

Urdu:
- Noto Nastaliq Urdu / Noto Sans Arabic

Requirements:
- RTL support
- Accessibility
- Government-grade readability

## Visual Language

Avoid:
- Generic agriculture imagery
- Marketplace appearance
- Outdated government portal style

Use:
- Laboratory imagery
- Inspection activities
- Regulatory processes
- Supply chain visuals
- Certification themes

## UI Direction

- Clean government digital service aesthetic
- Accessible components
- Data-first presentation
- Responsive bilingual experience
